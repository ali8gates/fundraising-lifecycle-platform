# Role alignment

I map this repository to the role this repo is pointed at: product ownership for data and integrations at a fundraising platform for educational institutions. I separate direct evidence from transfer. The code shows a prospect lifecycle and canonical CRM boundary. It does not prove experience the code does not show.

| Own an outcome number, not a roadmap | I worked from a fundraising problem, not a feature list. The school segment was below one percent of the overall fundraising goal and its pipeline was not visible. I shipped in about sixty days, deployed to roughly fourteen schools, and the six months after launch produced eighty thousand dollars in new giving from net new donors attributed to prospecting. The role's roughly ninety percent monthly completeness goal is the same kind of operating outcome. | `packages/db/prisma/schema.prisma` and `packages/shared/src/lifecycle.ts`. |

## Honest gaps and transfer

I have not run a vendor connector partnership at the scale of hundreds of institutions. I have not owned an integration surface across six different CRM families at once. I have not shown a production connection to the named donor CRM here, and I have not run joint partner roadmaps, sandboxes, versioning, or deprecation with external connector partners. I also did not build a flat file import path, complete gift workflow, alert delivery, role-based authorization, or end-user reporting surface.

The transfer is narrower. I have worked through a directional sync failure where the visible transport result was not the business result. I separated canonical entity meaning from source fields, made household ambiguity a safety boundary, used configuration profiles for customer variance, and added reconciliation so failure becomes detectable. I also shipped the supporting product and code, from public-signal ingestion through a usable fundraising lifecycle. Those are foundations for the larger integration scope, not a claim that I have already operated its full partner and CRM coverage.
